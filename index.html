The Studio

Design that stays livable, long after the styling fades.

Divine Interior is a Pune-based design and execution studio working across residences, offices and retail spaces. We start every project the same way — by understanding how a space is actually going to be used, not just how it should photograph.

Our team carries a project from the first site visit through layout, material selection, 3D visualisation, vendor coordination and execution — so the person who designs your home is the same person accountable for how it's built.

How We Work

Six stages, one point of contact.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A site visit and conversation to understand your space, budget and how you actually live or work.

  2. 02

    Layout & Planning

    Measured drawings and space planning, refined with you over 1–2 weeks until the layout is right.

  3. 03

    Design & 3D Visualisation

    Materials, finishes and furniture are finalised alongside 3D renders, so nothing is a surprise on-site.

  4. 04

    Vendor Finalisation

    We assemble and brief the contractors and vendors best suited to your specific project.

  5. 05

    Execution

    Site work proceeds in sequence with regular site visits, typically over 90–150 days.

  6. 06

    Handover

    A final walkthrough, snag-list closure, and the keys handed back to you.
